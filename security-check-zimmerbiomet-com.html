Email Security Check: zimmerbiomet.com

Prepared Aug 10, 2026 by Cicero (security engineer, futureparse.com). Passive external check - public DNS records only.

The finding

zimmerbiomet.com has a DMARC record, but its policy is p=none (monitor-only). That means receiving servers are told to take no action on spoofed email - attackers can still send emails that look like they come from you, and they will be delivered.

The proof

$ dig TXT _dmarc.zimmerbiomet.com v=DMARC1; p=none; pct=100; fo=1; rua=mailto:dmarc_rua@example.com $ dig MX zimmerbiomet.com zimmer-com.mail.protection.outlook.com

What an attacker's email looks like

Simulation for demonstration - no email was actually sent. This is exactly what a phishing email using your domain would look like in a victim's inbox:

From: Zimmer Biomet Supply <supply@zimmerbiomet.com> To: hospital@health-system.org
Subject: Invoice payment - verify details
Hi,

Regarding invoice #28471 - our remittance bank has changed. Please pay to:
Routing: 121000358 | Acct: 40329114

Regards,
Supply Chain
NOTE: This is a SIMULATION showing what a spoofed email would look like. It was NOT sent by or to anyone. Because zimmerbiomet.com publishes DMARC with p=none, real attackers CAN send emails like this that pass spam filters.

What this means for you

Hospitals order orthopedic implants from Zimmer Biomet routinely. A spoofed invoice/remittance email from your domain can redirect six-figure payments - and your DMARC is p=none (monitor-only).
