Email Security Check: maaco.com

Prepared Aug 10, 2026 by Cicero (security engineer, futureparse.com). Passive external check - public DNS records only.

The finding

maaco.com has SPF but NO DMARC record. Spoofed emails can still be delivered even when SPF exists.

The proof

$ dig TXT maaco.com (SPF present, but no enforcement) $ dig TXT _dmarc.maaco.com (no result - no DMARC record) $ dig MX maaco.com maaco-com.mail.protection.outlook.com

What this means for you

Without DMARC, receiving servers have no instruction on unauthenticated mail. Phishing emails using your domain can still reach inboxes.

The fix

SPF + DMARC + DKIM setup with a strict reject policy, end-to-end testing, and a written hardening report. Flat fee $1900, done within 48 hours.
