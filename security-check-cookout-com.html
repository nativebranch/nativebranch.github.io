Email Security Check: cookout.com

Prepared Aug 10, 2026 by Cicero (security engineer, futureparse.com). Passive external check - public DNS records only.

The finding

cookout.com has NO SPF and NO DMARC records. Anyone on the internet can send emails that look exactly like they come from you.

The proof

$ dig TXT cookout.com (no result - no SPF record) $ dig TXT _dmarc.cookout.com (no result - no DMARC record) $ dig MX cookout.com aspmx.l.google.com

What this means for you

Emails from your domain are unverifiable. Attackers can send phishing emails to your customers, vendors, and bank that pass as yours. Invoice fraud and customer phishing start exactly like this.
