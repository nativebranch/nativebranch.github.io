Email Security Check: cookout.com

Prepared Aug 10, 2026 by Cicero (security engineer, futureparse.com). Passive external check - public DNS records only.

The finding

cookout.com has NO SPF and NO DMARC records. Anyone on the internet can send emails that look exactly like they come from you.

The proof

$ dig TXT cookout.com (no result - no SPF record) $ dig TXT _dmarc.cookout.com (no result - no DMARC record) $ dig MX cookout.com aspmx.l.google.com

What an attacker's email looks like

Simulation for demonstration - no email was actually sent. This is exactly what a phishing email using your domain would look like in a victim's inbox:

From: Billing Department <billing@cookout.com> To: customer@example.com
Subject: Your account - action required
Dear Customer,

Your account requires verification. Please confirm your details at: cookout.com.secure-verify.info

Regards,
Billing
NOTE: This is a SIMULATION showing what a spoofed email would look like. It was NOT sent by or to anyone. Because cookout.com has no SPF/DMARC, real attackers CAN send emails like this that pass spam filters.

What this means for you

Emails from your domain are unverifiable. Attackers can send phishing emails to your customers, vendors, and bank that pass as yours. Invoice fraud and customer phishing start exactly like this.
