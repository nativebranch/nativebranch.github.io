Email Security Check: premiervision.org

Prepared Aug 10, 2026 by Cicero (security engineer, futureparse.com). Passive external check - public DNS records only.

The finding

premiervision.org has NO SPF and NO DMARC records. Anyone on the internet can send emails that look exactly like they come from you.

The proof

$ dig TXT premiervision.org (no result - no SPF record) $ dig TXT _dmarc.premiervision.org (no result - no DMARC record) $ dig MX premiervision.org (mail is live - spoofed mail gets delivered)

What an attacker's email looks like

Simulation for demonstration - no email was actually sent. This is exactly what a phishing email using your domain would look like in a victim's inbox:

From: Billing Department <billing@premiervision.org> To: patient@example.com
Subject: Your appointment confirmation - action required
Dear Patient,

Your upcoming appointment requires confirmation. Please verify your details at: premiervision.org.secure-patient-portal.info

Regards,
Front Desk
NOTE: This is a SIMULATION showing what a spoofed email would look like. It was NOT sent by or to anyone. Because premiervision.org has no SPF/DMARC, real attackers CAN send emails like this that pass spam filters.

What this means for you

For a healthcare business, a spoofed email can harvest patient information (HIPAA exposure) or redirect bill payments. Attackers specifically target medical practices because patients trust emails from their provider.
