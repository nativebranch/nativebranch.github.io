Email Security Check: eliteyecare.com

Prepared Aug 10, 2026 by Cicero (security engineer, futureparse.com). Passive external check - public DNS records only.

The finding

eliteyecare.com has NO SPF and NO DMARC records. Anyone on the internet can send emails that look exactly like they come from you.

The proof

$ dig TXT eliteyecare.com (no result - no SPF record) $ dig TXT _dmarc.eliteyecare.com (no result - no DMARC record) $ dig MX eliteyecare.com smtp.google.com
