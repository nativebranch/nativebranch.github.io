Email Security Check: davidsfinejewelryllc.com

Prepared Aug 10, 2026 by Cicero (security engineer, futureparse.com). Passive external check - public DNS records only.

The finding

davidsfinejewelryllc.com has NO SPF and NO DMARC records. Anyone on the internet can send emails that look exactly like they come from you.

The proof

$ dig TXT davidsfinejewelryllc.com (no result - no SPF record) $ dig TXT _dmarc.davidsfinejewelryllc.com (no result - no DMARC record) $ dig MX davidsfinejewelryllc.com aspmx.l.google.com

What this means for you

For a jewelry business, invoice fraud is the #1 attack: a fake invoice email from your domain to a customer or vendor can redirect a real payment. Without SPF/DMARC, your customers can also be phished as you - damaging the trust your brand depends on.
