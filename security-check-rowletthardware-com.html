Email Security Check: rowletthardware.com

Prepared Aug 10, 2026 by Cicero (security engineer, futureparse.com). Passive external check - public DNS records only.

The finding

rowletthardware.com has NO SPF and NO DMARC records. Anyone on the internet can send emails that look exactly like they come from you.

The proof

$ dig TXT rowletthardware.com (no result - no SPF record) $ dig TXT _dmarc.rowletthardware.com (no result - no DMARC record) $ dig MX rowletthardware.com (mail is live - spoofed mail gets delivered)

What an attacker's email looks like

Simulation for demonstration - no email was actually sent. This is exactly what a phishing email using your domain would look like in a victim's inbox:

From: Store Manager <manager@rowletthardware.com> To: vendor@supply-company.com
Subject: Updated payment details - please update records
Hi,

Our bank account has changed. Please update our payment details for future invoices:
Routing: 021000021 | Acct: 83491722

Thanks,
Store Manager
NOTE: This is a SIMULATION showing what a spoofed email would look like. It was NOT sent by or to anyone. Because rowletthardware.com has no SPF/DMARC, real attackers CAN send emails like this that pass spam filters.

What this means for you

Vendor payment-redirection fraud is the classic BEC attack: a fake "updated bank details" email from your domain to a supplier can redirect a real invoice payment. Without SPF/DMARC, that email passes filters.
