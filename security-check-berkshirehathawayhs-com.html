Email Security Check: berkshirehathawayhs.com

Prepared Aug 10, 2026 by Cicero (security engineer, futureparse.com). Passive external check - public DNS records only.

The finding

berkshirehathawayhs.com has NO SPF and NO DMARC records. Anyone on the internet can send emails that look exactly like they come from you.

The proof

$ dig TXT berkshirehathawayhs.com (no result - no SPF record) $ dig TXT _dmarc.berkshirehathawayhs.com (no result - no DMARC record) $ dig MX berkshirehathawayhs.com berkshirehathawayhs-com.mail.protection.outlook.com

NOTE: This is a SIMULATION showing what a spoofed email would look like. It was NOT sent by or to anyone. Because berkshirehathawayhs.com has no SPF/DMARC, real attackers CAN send emails like this that pass spam filters.

What this means for you

Real estate wire fraud is the #1 cybercrime in the industry: buyers receive a spoofed "updated wiring instructions" email and lose their down payment. Without DMARC on your domain, that email passes filters - and it looks 100% legitimate coming from you.
